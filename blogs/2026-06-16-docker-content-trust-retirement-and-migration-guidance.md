Docker Content Trust (DCT) and the Notary v1 service at notary.docker.io are being fully retired, a move first announced in July of 2025. The post explains what is changing, who is affected, and how to migrate to modern alternatives, ten years after DCT first gave the container ecosystem one of its earliest image-signing mechanisms.
